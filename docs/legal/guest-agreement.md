# CampShare Guest Agreement

This Guest Agreement applies to every person who books a Vehicle on CampShare. It supplements the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) — in case of conflict between this Agreement and the Terms, this Agreement prevails for Guest-specific matters.

By creating a Booking, you agree to be bound by this Agreement.

---

## 1. Guest eligibility

To book a Vehicle on CampShare you must:

- Meet the general eligibility criteria in clause 3 of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms);
- Be at least the minimum driver age set by the Listing (18, 21, or 25);
- Hold a valid full or restricted NZ driver licence, or a valid overseas driver licence accepted by NZ Transport Agency for the duration of the Booking;
- Successfully complete Stripe Identity KYC before your first Booking;
- Provide a valid payment method that supports both the Booking charge and the NZ$500 Security Deposit pre-authorisation.

If you provide an overseas licence, an official English translation may be required at handover. The Host may refuse the Vehicle at handover if your licence does not match the documentation you provided.

> [!review] Confirm correct treatment of overseas licences — NZTA accepts most for up to 12 months but with English-translation rules; CGA implications if a Host refuses handover on translation grounds.

## 2. Booking confirmation and payment

- Bookings are charged in full at confirmation. For Instant Book Listings, this is immediate; for Request to Book Listings, this occurs on Host acceptance (or your card is released if the Host declines).
- The NZ$500 Security Deposit (or higher amount displayed at checkout for some Vehicles) is pre-authorised at confirmation. It is released within 7 days of Vehicle return absent a damage claim.
- All amounts are charged via Stripe; CampShare does not store your card number.

## 3. Cancellation by Guest

The Cancellation Policy `standard_v1` applies. See [/legal/cancellation](/legal/cancellation) for full detail. Summary:

- **More than 7 days** before pickup — 100% refund of the nightly rate. Guest service fee is non-refundable.
- **2 to 7 days** before pickup — 50% refund of the nightly rate.
- **Less than 48 hours** before pickup — no refund.

The Security Deposit is always released in full when a Booking is cancelled (whichever party cancels).

## 4. Pickup and return

At pickup you must:

- Present, in person, the driver licence you registered with;
- Inspect the Vehicle with the Host;
- Photograph the Vehicle's exterior, interior, fuel level, and odometer (the in-app handover checklist captures these);
- Receive the Host's contact number for emergency use.

At return you must:

- Return the Vehicle to the agreed location at the agreed time;
- Return it in the same condition, fuel level, and cleanliness as at pickup (subject to fair wear and tear and any cleaning fee disclosed in the Listing);
- Be present (or have a delegated adult present) for the return inspection.

Late return without prior agreement may attract an extra-night charge plus any costs incurred by the Host or the next Booking.

## 5. While you have the Vehicle

You must:

- Drive lawfully and not operate the Vehicle while impaired by alcohol, drugs, or fatigue;
- Comply with all NZ road rules and the Vehicle's stated limitations (e.g. transmission type, off-road / gravel-road restrictions, height/weight clearances);
- Only allow named, KYC-verified drivers to drive the Vehicle. Additional drivers must be added to the Booking in-app before pickup.
- Not use the Vehicle for any unlawful purpose, including subletting, paid transport, racing, or off-road use unless specifically allowed by the Listing;
- Notify the Host immediately of any accident, damage, mechanical fault, or warning light;
- Notify NZ Police of any accident as required by law.

## 6. Damage, fines, and tolls

You are liable for:

- Damage to the Vehicle occurring during your Booking, up to the insurance excess (default NZ$2,500; reduced if you purchase the Excess Reduction Add-on);
- Damage caused by your negligence, wilful conduct, or breach of this Agreement, up to the full repair or replacement cost;
- Any traffic infringement, parking ticket, toll, or speeding fine incurred during your Booking;
- Any cleaning beyond ordinary wear (charged at the rate disclosed in the Listing or, absent disclosure, at NZ$80/hour up to a $300 cap).

CampShare may charge the Security Deposit and (if necessary) the Guest's saved payment method for any of the above following the dispute workflow.

## 7. Insurance

Your Booking is covered by `[INSURER]` during the rental period. The cover provides:

- Comprehensive cover for damage to the Vehicle subject to the excess noted above;
- Public-liability cover of NZ`[$5M — TBC]` for damage you cause to third parties;
- 24/7 roadside assistance via `[AA / partner]`.

The cover does **not** apply if:

- You allow an unverified driver to drive the Vehicle;
- The driver is under the minimum age set by the Listing;
- The Vehicle is operated outside the conditions of the Listing (e.g. on a track or beach, towing in excess of the rated capacity, off-road where prohibited);
- The driver is impaired by alcohol or drugs above legal limits;
- The Vehicle is used for any unlawful purpose.

In any of these cases you are personally liable for the full cost of repair or replacement plus any third-party claim.

## 8. Disputes

If you believe a Host has misrepresented a Vehicle, failed to provide it in the condition described, or is making an unjustified damage claim, raise a dispute via `/dashboard/bookings/[id]/dispute` within 7 days of Booking completion. See clause 10 of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) for the dispute process.

## 9. Reviews

You may review each Host after Booking completion. Reviews must be honest, factual, and free of personal attacks. CampShare may remove reviews that breach the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use).

## 10. Suspension and termination

CampShare may suspend or terminate your Guest account immediately for:

- Material breach of this Agreement;
- Repeated cancellations or low Host review scores below our published thresholds;
- KYC failure or evidence of identity misrepresentation;
- Any conduct that endangers a Host, Vehicle, or third party.

You may close your Guest account at any time. Closure does not relieve you of obligations relating to Bookings already in progress.

## 11. Variation

CampShare may vary this Agreement from time to time. Material variations will be notified to Guests by email at least 14 days before they take effect.

## 12. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by NZ law per the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).
