# CampShare Host Agreement

This Host Agreement applies to every person who lists a Vehicle on CampShare. It supplements the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) — in case of conflict between this Agreement and the Terms, this Agreement prevails for Host-specific matters.

By submitting a Host application or publishing a Listing, you agree to be bound by this Agreement.

## 1. Host eligibility

To list a Vehicle on CampShare you must:

- Meet the general eligibility criteria in clause 3 of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms);
- Be the registered legal owner of the Vehicle, or hold the owner's written authority to list it;
- Hold a current Warrant of Fitness (WoF) or Certificate of Fitness (CoF) for the Vehicle as required by NZ law;
- Hold current vehicle registration (rego);
- Hold the personal insurance required by your own arrangements outside any CampShare cover;
- Have a NZ bank account suitable for receiving Host payouts via Stripe Connect.

## 2. Listing standards

Each Listing must:

- Accurately describe the Vehicle (make, model, year, seats, sleeps, transmission, fuel type, key amenities);
- Use only photographs you own or have permission to use, taken of the actual Vehicle being listed;
- State the nightly rate, cleaning fee (if any), and any Add-ons clearly;
- State the pickup location accurately (we display this at neighbourhood resolution publicly and at exact-address resolution to confirmed Guests only);
- State the minimum driver age tier (18, 21 or 25);
- Disclose any unusual conditions (e.g. manual-only transmission, no pets, no smoking, restrictions on gravel-road use).

You must not:

- List a Vehicle that is not roadworthy, currently registered, or covered by current insurance;
- List a Vehicle you do not control;
- Misrepresent the Vehicle's condition, age, mileage, or features;
- Place external contact details in the Listing or first-message thread to circumvent CampShare fees (see clause 9).

CampShare may remove any Listing that breaches these standards.

## 3. Pricing and fees

- You set your own nightly rate and (optionally) cleaning fee, Add-ons, and security-deposit top-up.
- CampShare takes a **5% Host commission** on the nightly rate, deducted from each payout.
- A 15% GST applies to CampShare's commission. If you are GST-registered, you are responsible for accounting for GST on the nightly rate paid by the Guest; CampShare provides a payout statement to support that.

If you wish to change your rate, you may do so at any time. The change does not affect Bookings already confirmed.

## 4. Calendar, availability, and acceptance

- You must keep your calendar up-to-date. You may sync external calendars (iCal import) to prevent double-booking.
- If your Listing is set to **Instant Book** and the Guest meets the eligibility criteria (verified, no recent cancellations, meets minimum driver age), the Booking is confirmed automatically.
- If your Listing is set to **Request to Book**, you have **24 hours** to accept or decline. Failure to respond within 24 hours counts as a decline and may reduce your "Responds Reliably" status.

## 5. Vehicle handover

At each Booking, you must:

- Provide the Vehicle in safe, clean, working condition, fuelled and watered as represented;
- Verify the Guest's driver licence matches the name on the verified CampShare account;
- Walk through the Vehicle's operation with the Guest;
- Photograph the Vehicle's exterior, interior, fuel level, and odometer at handover (CampShare provides a handover checklist);
- Provide your contact number for emergency use during the rental.

At return, you must:

- Re-photograph the Vehicle in the same conditions;
- Note any damage in writing within the in-app dispute thread within the 7-day dispute window.

## 6. Cancellation by Host

Cancelling a confirmed Booking other than for a valid safety, compliance, or force-majeure reason will result in:

- Full refund to the Guest;
- A Host penalty equal to 10% of the booking value (capped at NZ$200);
- Possible loss of Super Host status if cancellations exceed our published threshold.

Valid cancellation reasons that avoid penalty are limited to: documented Vehicle breakdown, documented force-majeure event, evidence of Guest misrepresentation that would void insurance.

## 8. Security deposit

A NZ$500 Security Deposit is pre-authorised on the Guest's payment method at Booking and released by Stripe within 7 days of Vehicle return absent a dispute. You may set a higher deposit per Listing where the Vehicle's value justifies it (subject to a cap of NZ`[$2,000 — TBC]`).

Damage claims must be raised within the 7-day dispute window. CampShare's adjudication is binding on release or withholding of the deposit but does not preclude either party from pursuing further civil remedies between themselves.

## 9. Off-platform transactions

You must conduct all Bookings, payments, and communications through CampShare. Specifically you must not:

- Solicit or accept off-platform payment from a Guest;
- Discount or refund off-platform to avoid CampShare's commission;
- Move messaging off-platform until a Booking is confirmed (post-confirmation phone contact is permitted and encouraged for handover).

Breach of this clause is a material breach of this Agreement and may result in immediate termination plus invoice for the CampShare commission that would have applied.

## 10. Reviews

You may review each Guest after Booking completion. Reviews must be honest, factual, and free of personal attacks. CampShare may remove reviews that breach the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use).

## 11. Tax

You are solely responsible for:

- Declaring rental income to IRD;
- Registering for and accounting for GST where your annual rental turnover (across all platforms and outside platforms) exceeds NZ$60,000;
- Keeping accurate records for tax purposes (CampShare provides annual payout statements to support this).

CampShare does not withhold tax. If we become subject to a withholding-tax obligation in future (for example under new platform-economy rules), we will give you notice.

## 12. Suspension and termination

CampShare may suspend or terminate your Host account immediately for:

- Material breach of this Agreement;
- Failure of safety, compliance, or insurance requirements;
- Repeated cancellations or low Guest review scores below our published thresholds;
- Any conduct that brings CampShare into disrepute.

You may close your Host account at any time. Closure does not relieve you of obligations relating to Bookings already in progress.

## 13. Variation

CampShare may vary this Agreement from time to time. Material variations will be notified to Hosts by email at least 14 days before they take effect. Continued listing after the effective date constitutes acceptance.

## 14. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by NZ law per the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).
