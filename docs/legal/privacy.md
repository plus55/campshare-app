# CampShare Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how **CampShare Ltd** (NZBN `[TBD]`) collects, uses, discloses, retains and protects personal information. It complies with the **Privacy Act 2020 (NZ)**, including **Information Privacy Principle (IPP) 3A** which came into force on **1 May 2026** and applies to personal information collected indirectly.

## 2. Personal information we collect — indirectly (IPP 3A)

From **1 May 2026**, IPP 3A requires us to take reasonable steps to make you aware when we collect personal information about you from a source other than you. The categories below are collected indirectly. By disclosing this in our Privacy Policy and in confirmation emails at the first relevant event, we discharge our IPP 3A obligation.

| Indirect source | What we receive | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Stripe (Stripe Connect, Stripe Identity)** | KYC verification result, identity-document validity status, payment-method validity, payout-failure signals | At Guest KYC and at each payment / payout event |
| **Other Users** | Reviews and ratings about you; reports or disputes about you | Whenever a User reviews or reports |
| **Cloudflare Turnstile** | Bot-protection signals | At signup and at each Booking creation |
| **Cloudflare logs** | Edge access logs (IP, user agent) | On every request |
| **Mapbox** | Geocoding of Listing addresses | When Host creates or updates a Listing |
| **Insurance underwriter `[INSURER]`** | Eligibility decisions and claim outcomes for Bookings you are a party to | At Booking and at any claim |

## 3. Why we collect personal information

We use your personal information to:

1. Operate the CampShare marketplace (display Listings, accept Bookings, process payments, send messages, publish reviews);
2. Verify identity and prevent fraud (Stripe Identity, Turnstile, manual review);
3. Comply with our legal obligations (tax, AML/CFT to the extent applicable, lawful information requests);
4. Communicate with you about Bookings and your account (transactional);
5. Communicate with you about CampShare news, features, and offers (marketing — only with your consent at signup; you can opt out any time);
6. Improve the Platform (anonymised or aggregated analytics);
7. Resolve disputes and adjudicate Security Deposits;
8. Defend ourselves in legal proceedings or respond to lawful requests from regulators or law enforcement.

## 4. When we share your personal information

We share personal information only as described below. We never sell personal information.

| Recipient | What | Purpose |
|---|---|---|

| **Other Users** | Name, profile photo, public review history, the messages you send them | Operating the marketplace (Host sees the Guest's name and KYC-verified status; both see each other's review history). |
| **Tax authorities** | Bank details, Booking history, name, NZBN where Host is a registered business | IRD compliance. |
| **Law enforcement or regulator** | As legally required | Compliance with valid legal process. |

We do not transfer personal information overseas except as required to operate the services listed above. Stripe stores identity documents in the United States; Cloudflare, Mapbox and Resend operate globally. Where personal information is sent overseas, we rely on the recipient's compliance with the OECD Privacy Framework and equivalent protections.

- **Analytics cookies** — Cloudflare Web Analytics + `[PostHog | Plausible — TBD per [[14-Analytics-KPIs]]]` to understand usage patterns. Opt-in via the cookie banner.

You can manage your preferences via the cookie banner that appears on first visit and from the footer "Cookie preferences" link thereafter.

## 6. Security

We protect personal information by:

- TLS encryption in transit;
- Encryption at rest (Cloudflare R2, Cloudflare D1);
- Access controls — only authorised CampShare personnel can access production data, and only as needed;
- Annual review of access lists;
- Stripe stores all card numbers and KYC document images (CampShare never sees raw card numbers or raw identity documents);
- Bot protection (Cloudflare Turnstile) on signup and Booking creation;
- Rate limits on authentication and Booking endpoints.

No system is perfectly secure. If we suffer a notifiable privacy breach (under section 117 of the Privacy Act 2020), we will notify the Office of the Privacy Commissioner and affected individuals as soon as practicable.

## 7. How long we retain personal information

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account profile | While account is active; 24 months after closure unless legally required to retain longer |
| Bookings, payments, payouts | 7 years from Booking completion (NZ tax record-keeping requirement) |
| KYC documents | Stored by Stripe per Stripe's retention policy (we do not store images) |
| Messages | While account is active; 24 months after closure |
| Reviews | Indefinite (anonymised after account closure) |
| Server / access logs | 30 days for security logs; aggregated indefinitely |

## 8. Your rights under the Privacy Act 2020

You have the right to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you (Privacy Principle 6) — make a request to `privacy@campshare.co.nz`;
- **Request correction** of any incorrect personal information (Principle 7);
- **Be notified** of indirect collection per IPP 3A — this Policy plus per-event reminders fulfil that obligation;
- **Complain** to us first, and if unresolved, to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at [privacy.org.nz](https://www.privacy.org.nz/).

We will respond to access and correction requests within 20 working days as required by the Act.

## 9. Marketing

You will only receive marketing communications from us if you opted in. You can opt out at any time by:

- Clicking the unsubscribe link in any marketing email;
- Updating your preferences in account settings;
- Emailing `privacy@campshare.co.nz`.

Opting out of marketing does not opt you out of transactional Booking communications.

## 10. Children

CampShare is not directed to children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact `privacy@campshare.co.nz` and we will delete it.

## 11. Changes to this Policy

We may amend this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified to Users by email and on first login after the change. The "version" and "effective" date at the top of this page indicate the current version.
