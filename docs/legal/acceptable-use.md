# CampShare Acceptable Use Policy

This Policy sets out what you can and cannot do on CampShare. It forms part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) — breach is grounds for content removal, account suspension, or account termination.

---

## Don't do this

You must not, directly or indirectly:

### Misrepresentation and fraud

- Create a CampShare account using a false identity, a stolen identity, or another person's identity;
- Use a payment instrument you are not authorised to use;
- List a Vehicle you do not own or control;
- Misrepresent a Vehicle's condition, age, mileage, features, or location;
- Manipulate ratings or reviews (by buying them, exchanging them off-platform, threatening them, or posting fake reviews of your own Listings or competitors');
- Use Add-on selection or pricing to mislead a Guest about the total cost;
- Game the Instant Book eligibility criteria.

### Off-platform

- Solicit or accept payment off-platform to avoid CampShare's commission;
- Move Booking-related discussions, agreements, or transactions off-platform before a Booking is confirmed;
- Use CampShare's messaging system to advertise other rental services, products, or platforms.

### Safety and lawfulness

- Operate or allow operation of a Vehicle while impaired by alcohol or drugs;
- Use a Vehicle for any unlawful purpose, including transporting illegal goods, racing, paid passenger transport, or sub-letting;
- Drive a Vehicle off-road or on routes prohibited by the Listing;
- Allow an unverified or unlicensed driver to drive a Vehicle;
- Tamper with a Vehicle's safety systems, odometer, or registration.

### Harassment and harmful conduct

- Harass, threaten, intimidate, stalk, defame, or discriminate against another User;
- Post or send content that is sexually explicit, violent, hateful, or otherwise grossly offensive;
- Make any threat of violence or property damage;
- Out, dox, or otherwise disclose another User's private information.

### Discrimination

- Refuse a Booking, cancel a Booking, or otherwise treat a User differently on the basis of race, colour, ethnic or national origin, religious belief, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, marital status, family status, age, disability, or political opinion (Human Rights Act 1993).

Hosts may set lawful, neutral Listing rules (e.g. "no pets", "no smoking", "minimum age 25") provided they apply equally to all Guests.

### Technical and platform

- Attempt to gain unauthorised access to any part of CampShare or another User's account;
- Run automated scrapers, bots, or load-testing tools against CampShare without prior written permission;
- Interfere with CampShare's security features (Turnstile, rate-limits, KYC);
- Use CampShare in any way that imposes an unreasonable load on our infrastructure;
- Reverse-engineer or attempt to derive source code from any CampShare component.

### Intellectual property

- Copy, display, or distribute CampShare content without authorisation;
- Upload content (including photographs and copy) you do not own or have permission to use;
- Use CampShare branding except as authorised in writing.

### Sanctions and AML

- Use CampShare while subject to NZ or applicable international sanctions;
- Use CampShare to facilitate any transaction prohibited under the Anti-Money Laundering and Countering Financing of Terrorism Act 2009 to the extent applicable.

---

## Consequences

Breach of this Policy may result in:

- Removal of the offending content;
- A formal warning recorded against your account;
- Temporary suspension of your account;
- Permanent termination of your account;
- Forfeiture of any pending Host payout to the extent a Guest claim is sustained;
- Referral to law enforcement.

CampShare's decision on the appropriate consequence is at our reasonable discretion. We will notify you in writing of any suspension or termination and the reason for it, except where notification would prejudice an investigation or law-enforcement action.

## Appeals

You may appeal a suspension or termination by emailing `appeals@campshare.co.nz` within 14 days of the decision. Appeals are reviewed by a CampShare team member not involved in the original decision and resolved within 10 business days.

## Reporting

If you believe another User is breaching this Policy, report them via:

- The "Report user" link on their profile or in any message thread;
- Email to `safety@campshare.co.nz` with as much detail as you can provide.

We treat reports confidentially. We will not disclose the identity of a reporter to the subject of the report.
